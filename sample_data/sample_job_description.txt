Product Manager, AI Platform — Example Corp (fictional sample)

About the role
We're looking for a Product Manager to own our internal AI platform, which lets
product teams across the company ship LLM-powered features safely and quickly.

What you'll do
- Define the strategy and roadmap for the AI platform, balancing internal team needs
- Partner with engineering, data science, and legal on model evaluation and safety
- Run customer discovery with internal product teams and turn it into requirements
- Define success metrics and run experiments to measure adoption and quality
- Communicate priorities and tradeoffs to senior leadership

Requirements
- 4+ years of product management experience, ideally on technical or platform products
- Hands-on experience shipping products that use LLMs or machine learning
- Strong analytical skills; comfortable writing SQL and interpreting A/B test results
- Experience working closely with engineering on APIs or developer-facing products
- Excellent written communication

Nice to have
- Background in consulting or strategy
- Experience with evaluation frameworks for AI systems
